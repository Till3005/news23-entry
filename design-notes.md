# Design notes — THE WORLD, 23 November 2026

`entry.png` is a square newspaper front page for the make.fun `news-image-20261123` bounty.

## Composition

- **Masthead:** black banner with large serif `THE WORLD`, over a red date strip reading `MONDAY, 23 NOVEMBER 2026` so the target date is unmissable.
- **Grid:** six photo panels in a 2-row × 3-column layout, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
- **Caption bars:** each panel has a solid black caption strip at the bottom with a numbered red tab and a short uppercase headline. The bars separate image from text, keep type legible at thumbnail scale, and give the page its front-page rhythm.

## Why real dated headlines beat generic ones

Generic "ongoing thread" labels (winter strikes, tariffs, AI boom) could describe almost any day; they read as filler and give a judge nothing to anchor to the date. Named, dated events are specific and checkable: they prove the entry is about *this* news day, look like a real front page, and are harder to mistake for stock imagery. The captions are deliberately short so each is instantly scannable without inventing detail.
