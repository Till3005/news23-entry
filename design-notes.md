# Design notes — entry.png

The entry is a single broadsheet front page of a fictional paper called THE WORLD, its masthead set in a heavy serif across the full width, boxed by thin double rules.

Beneath the masthead runs a solid red date strip with "MONDAY, 23 NOVEMBER 2026" in white capitals, flanked by issue number, volume, and price details in small print.

The body is a strict six-panel photo grid, two rows by three columns, every panel pairing one photograph with a bold headline, a one-line caption, and a tiny credit tag.

Each panel carries a dated headline tied to a real event from late November 2026: a climate accord signing, a deep-space telescope image drop, a fusion ignition milestone, a decisive national election, a world-record athletics finish, and a markets-and-AI business story.

Headlines use a condensed sans for punch, while captions sit in a readable text serif; hairline rules and fixed gutters keep the grid locked to the page margins.

The palette stays restrained: warm newsprint off-white, near-black ink, and one red accent reserved for the date strip and small section labels.

A thin footer bar closes the page with folio number, a weather line, and continuation notices, echoing the masthead's double rules.
